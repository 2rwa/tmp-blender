# Rigid Sphere Impact 1000 Transparent V2

Higher-quality follow-up to `rigid-sphere-impact-1000`.

The containment walls are still passive rigid-body colliders, but are rendered as glass-like transparent surfaces so the debris remains contained without visually boxing in the scene.

- 1000 active rigid-body cubes
- one 22 kg impact sphere
- floor + three transparent physical walls
- open camera/front side
- 960 × 540
- 96 frames at 24 fps
- rigid-body substeps: 12
- solver iterations: 30
- MP4 render plus two .blend outputs

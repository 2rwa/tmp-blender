# Rigid Sphere Impact 1000

A Blender rigid-body experiment that drops one heavy sphere into a 10 × 10 × 10 stack of active rigid-body cubes.

- 1000 active rigid-body cubes
- one 18 kg impact sphere
- passive floor, side walls, and rear wall
- open camera side so debris can fly toward the viewer
- 72 frames at 24 fps
- MP4 render plus two .blend outputs

Outputs:

- `rigid-sphere-impact-1000.mp4`
- `poster.png`
- `rigid-sim.blend`
- `rigid-result.blend`
- `validation.json`
